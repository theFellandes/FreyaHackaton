MERIDIAN SAVINGS BANK - ARREARS COLLECTIONS POLICY (EXTRACT, VERSION 3, valid from 2026-10-01)
Owner: Collections Operations. Classification: internal / synthetic training data.
Only sections 4 to 9 are reproduced here. Sections 1-3 (scope, governance) and 10 (reporting) are omitted.

4. CALENDAR AND DEADLINES
4.1 Business days are Monday to Friday. The following bank holidays are not business days: 19 October 2026, 3 November 2026, 27 November 2026, 24 December 2026, 31 December 2026.
4.2 The promise deadline depends on the customer's days overdue (days_overdue in the account data):
      Band GREEN   1-10 days overdue    deadline: 10th business day after the call date    persuasion attempts: 1
      Band AMBER   11-30 days overdue   deadline: 6th business day after the call date     persuasion attempts: 2
      Band RED     31-60 days overdue   deadline: 3rd business day after the call date     persuasion attempts: 3
      (The former four-band model with a 90-day band was retired on 30 September 2026 and must not be used.)
4.3 A promise date has to be the call date or later, a business day, and not later than the deadline.
4.4 If the customer names a Saturday, a Sunday or a bank holiday which is not after the deadline, the next business day is offered. A day after the deadline: the latest recordable date is the deadline, it is offered; if the customer refuses that, it counts as a flat refusal (6.2).
4.5 A vague period such as "next week", "after payday" or "soon": ask once for the exact day. If the customer still gives none it counts as a flat refusal (6.2).
4.6 "Next <weekday>" is the first time that weekday occurs after the call date; "this <weekday>" is the one in the current week if it is today or later.
4.7 Dates are always calculated with the `now` value of the call, never the system clock.

5. AMOUNTS
5.1 The assistant asks for the full overdue amount (past_due) first. It never asks "how much can you pay". Every amount is proposed by the assistant.
5.2 The floor amount is min_due for a card and monthly_instalment for a personal loan and for vehicle finance.
5.3 If the customer says the full amount is too much, the floor is offered once, saying that the rest stays overdue. Refusal of the floor = flat refusal (6.2). Attempts 2 and 3 may name the floor again on the deadline.
5.4 An amount proposed by the customer from the floor up to the full amount (both included) is accepted. Below the floor: say it cannot be recorded and offer the floor once; if not accepted = flat refusal.

6. GETTING THE PROMISE
6.1 First ask, spoken exactly: "Can you settle this today?"
6.2 A flat refusal or a non-answer uses up one persuasion attempt. A "no" to paying today without a date and without an amount is a flat refusal. A date or an amount that the customer proposes is never a refusal; it is handled under 4 and 5.
     Attempt 1: propose the next business day after the call date, for the full overdue amount (or for the floor if the customer already said the full amount is too much).
     Attempt 2 (amber and red only): propose the floor amount on the deadline.
     Attempt 3 (red only): propose the floor amount on the next business day after the call date.
6.3 When the attempts are used up the assistant stops and does not repeat an offer that was refused: outcome declined / rejected.
6.4 Illness, bereavement or loss of a job: one short sentence of sympathy, no persuasion attempt, offer the care team. Accepted: transfer_to_agent with queue care_team, outcome escalated / care. Declined: outcome declined / vulnerable. This rule has priority over 6.2 and 8.3. If the customer then names a date or amount on their own, the promise is recorded under 4 and 5.
6.5 No discount, waiver, payment holiday or any other term that is not in this document may be offered.

7. CONFIRMATION AND RECORDING
7.1 The assistant reads back product, amount and date and asks "Shall I record this?". create_promise is called only after a clear yes. If date or amount change at this point, the new values are checked against 4 and 5 and read back again.
7.2 One promise per product. A recorded promise cannot be changed or cancelled in the same call; the customer can change it in online banking.
7.3 "Recorded" may only be said after create_promise returned ok = true. When it fails, there is NO second attempt: the assistant says that it could not record the promise and closes declined / system_fault.
7.4 After a recorded promise the assistant says exactly: "A confirmation will be sent to you by text message."

8. OTHER CUSTOMER STATEMENTS
8.1 "I already paid": payments can take two business days to show; no promise is taken; outcome settled.
8.2 "Not my debt" / "never had this product": no argument, no promise: declined / contested. A claim that the product was closed in the past is a dispute. 8.1 only covers a payment of this overdue amount.
8.3 Request for a new payment plan or lower instalments: no plan is described; transfer_to_agent queue payment_plans; outcome escalated / plan.
8.4 Request for a person: the assistant asks once whether it can help; if the customer insists: transfer_to_agent queue front_desk; outcome escalated / agent.
8.5 "Is this a scam?": the customer may verify the call by phoning the customer service number on their latest statement; the call continues.
8.6 Questions the tools cannot answer: say "I do not have that information"; never invent figures, dates or reasons.
8.7 If the customer says twice that they do not want to talk: declined / rejected; before identity is verified: unverified / declined_checks. 8.4 applies before and after verification.

9. TWO PRODUCTS AND CLOSING
9.1 get_account returns one or two products, each with its own past_due, days_overdue and floor. Band and deadline are determined per product; persuasion attempts are counted per product.
9.2 Before asking anything, the assistant states each overdue product in one sentence. Then it asks whether the customer can settle both today; if not, the products are taken one by one in the order get_account returned them.
9.3 All promises are read back together with one confirmation question; then create_promise is called once per product.
9.4 For every product without a promise add_note is called with the product_id and one reason from: rejected, vulnerable, contested, settled, system_fault. A dispute or "already paid" for one product does not end the call.
9.5 No notes at all are added on any transfer, neither before nor after it. Promises recorded before a transfer stay.
9.6 Closing: exactly one close_call, as the last tool call, after thanking the customer and saying goodbye. With at least one recorded promise: agreed (but see 9.5: a transfer closes as escalated with the transfer's reason). With no promise: if every product is noted settled -> settled; otherwise declined with the reason of the first product (get_account order) whose note is not settled.
9.7 Outcome and reason codes are listed in the code table (outcome_codes.csv).
